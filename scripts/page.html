About me

I am a PhD candidate at the X-LANCE Lab, School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Kai Yu.

My research focuses on speech synthesis, speech tokenization, and speech language models, with an emphasis on robust, efficient, and controllable speech generation.

I am currently a research intern at rednote HiLab (dots). Previously, I received my bachelor’s degree from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2022, advised by Prof. Dongjing Miao.

Experience

Sep 2025 – Present

rednote (Xiaohongshu)

Research Intern · HiLab (dots studio)

Work on industrial speech synthesis, speech editing, full-duplex models, and the speech representations that underpin them.

dots.tts · Core Contributor

Developed an industrial text-to-speech foundation model: a 2B-parameter continuous autoregressive system supporting expressive voice cloning, multilingual synthesis, and low-latency streaming generation.

dots.tts.edit · Project Lead and Primary Contributor

Led the development of an end-to-end model for precise speech editing. The system supports local text, emotion, prosody, pause, free-form style, and compositional edits while preserving the surrounding speech and speaker identity. The project also introduces doteBench for evaluating instruction following, local preservation, and audio quality.
